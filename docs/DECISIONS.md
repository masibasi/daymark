# Daymark Decision Log

## Product

- **Today is home.** The primary question is what to do now.
- **The Day Orbit leads Today.** Completion context and the compact history calendar appear before deadlines and task rows, so accomplishment is part of orientation rather than an afterthought.
- **Today’s header is factual, not motivational.** It shows the selected date. The mock weather line was removed on 2026-10-06 (owner decision: real weather would need location permission and an API for little value).
- **Today can revisit another date.** Selecting a day in the compact history calendar changes the visible tasks and completion record without changing the scheduling calendar's role.
- **The history calendar defaults to collapsed on phone, expanded on desktop.** Collapsed shows just the Sun–Sat week containing the selected date; expanded shows the full month. A quiet toggle in the card header switches between them, and the prev/next arrows move by week or month to match. The user's choice always wins over the width-based default once they toggle it.
- **Calendar is primary navigation, second to Today.** Its role is viewing events and importing them as Tasks (see the time-blocking pause below).
- **User-facing name is "Folders" (owner decision 2026-10-01; Korean 폴더).** It was "Deadlines"; the owner plans loosely ("a J who acts like a P, or a P who acts like a J"): keep work in containers and pull items into a day as needed. A folder may or may not have a deadline. The code entity stays `Project` (route `/projects`, store names unchanged). Today deadline cards expand in place into a panel below the strip (next 4 open steps with a ☀︎ Today toggle; "Open" goes to full detail).
- **Projects are persistent.** They remain visible regardless of Today's selection.
- **Deadline work is the primary differentiator.** Project urgency, progress, and the next actionable steps take priority over becoming a general-purpose list manager.
- **Deadline previews stay compact.** Today shows the most urgent three and links to the rest.
- **Deadline urgency lives in the label, not the card.** Deadline cards stay neutral (`colors.paper` / `colors.line`) at every urgency step; only the `D−n` label darkens from muted, to soft ink, to full ink, to danger red as the due date approaches. This was a deliberate reversal of an earlier tinted/warm/rose card treatment, which the product owner found too loud for a calm surface.
- **Task is not CalendarEvent.** External commitments do not become todos.
- **TimeBlock links intent to time.** A Task can have zero, one, or many blocks.
- **Completion uses Day Orbit.** Category identity is preserved through stable ring segments.
- **Watercolor is concentrated in the Day Mark.** Its pale wash and restrained surface provide organic character; the rest of the app keeps a crisp black/white foundation instead of becoming full-screen glassmorphism.
- **No social or gamification in V1.** The emotional reward is the visible record of finished work.
- **Neutral black/white foundation.** Light mode is white, dark mode is near-black; category colors carry the visual energy instead of beige or warm neutrals.
- **Today includes contextual quick add.** A daily task can be created without leaving Today. (Superseded: see per-list input below.)
- **Rescheduling matters more than arbitrary lists.** Daymark should support moving an unfinished Task to another day; broad user-defined list hierarchies are not required for the core loop.
- **Task actions live beside the Task.** The `…` menu moves unfinished work to tomorrow, a chosen date, or off the displayed day without duplicating it.
- **Day Mark directions are compared at equal states.** The study route shows four visual approaches at 0/25/50/100 percent; its existence does not select a final treatment.
- **Day Mark variants share one renderer.** `DayOrbit` draws Soft ribbon, Glass vessel, Watercolor wash, and the original baseline from the same `selectDayOrbit` segments. The study route (`/mark-lab`) shows each at Today size, at 22px calendar size, and on a dark panel, and its "Use on Today" control switches the live Today, history, and month marks for the session, for reference and comparison. New variants use softened per-category `mark` tokens, and the wash bleeds inward from each category's own arc so colors touch but never mix or overlap.
- **Watercolor wash is the shipped default.** After reviewing the study route, the product owner chose Watercolor wash over the ribbon, glass, and original baseline treatments: a ribbon band in softened per-category `mark` tokens, with a pale wash bleeding inward from each category's own arc. Colors touch but never mix. Slow, near-imperceptible breathing is reserved for the large Today mark only; calendar-size and history marks stay static. The lab remains reachable from Today for reference, not as an active decision point.
- **Deadline attention begins per Project.** Seven days is the default; Project Detail offers 3/7/14/30 days for when the `D−n` label starts to darken. D−3 and D−1 still intensify the label further in this prototype.
- **Calendar integration stays explicit.** Calendar events provide time context; Events do not silently become todos; the user imports one explicitly with "Add to <day>".
- **The Today time rail and "reserve time" tray were tried and parked.** A V0 study added a compact 8 AM–9 PM strip on Today plus a per-Task "Reserve time" tray, so a Task could pick up a TimeBlock without a trip to the Calendar tab. The product owner reviewed it and found the interaction didn't resonate; it has been removed (preserved in git history at commit `c673d1b`). Time-blocking itself is now paused (below).
- **Project detail remains focused on mobile.** V0 keeps full-screen project detail. A desktop side panel is preferred over an expanding card because it preserves the deadline overview while allowing quick edits; this is a later interaction refinement.

- **Phone Today is a compact summary plus a swipeable Tasks | Schedule pager (owner choice).** The big Day Mark and history calendar collapse into one summary row by default so tasks are on the first screen; the day's calendar events live on a second page instead of crowding Today.
- **Event import defaults and Calendar-tab import (owner decision 2026-10-01).** Tapping an event in the Calendar tab opens the same Add affordance as Today's Schedule, adding a Task for the event's own date. The default list is one named Schedule/Calendar, else the list last used for an event import; if neither exists the primary action creates a "Schedule" list and adds in one step.
- **Events become Tasks only through an explicit "Add to Today".** Tapping an event offers the lists; choosing one creates a normal Task for the displayed day with `sourceEventId` set to the event id. One task per event per day; the event then shows "Added". External events are never converted automatically, and Tasks, TimeBlocks and CalendarEvents stay separate entities.
- **Lists have twelve colors.** The palette grew from six to twelve distinct hues; existing colorKeys are unchanged.

## Technical

- **Expo + React Native Web.** One TypeScript UI foundation across phone and web.
- **Expo Router.** Routes remain addressable on web and portable to native navigation.
- **Zustand for V0.** Small in-memory state with explicit domain actions.
- **Selectors own derivation.** Components render data; they do not duplicate domain math.
- **`completedAt` is the only completion-history source.** Scheduling never implies completion.
- **Mock calendar provider first, then read-only ICS feeds.** The mock provider now only backs "Load sample data".
- **Time-blocking is paused (owner decision 2026-10-01).** Daymark-only TimeBlocks don't appear in the owner's real Google/Apple calendar, so they were not useful. The Calendar tab is view + import: the schedule button, SchedulePanel and select-then-tap-slot flow were removed, existing TimeBlocks stay visible and removable, and no new ones can be created. Revisit if/when calendar write-back exists. Calendar drag-and-drop remains out of scope.
- **Drag-and-drop is allowed on Today, for Tasks only (owner request 2026-09-30).** Long-press (~300 ms) a task row to reorder it or move it to another list. This reverses the earlier V0 "no drag-and-drop" rule for this one purpose; Calendar scheduling and everything else stay select-then-tap. `Task.order` (0-based, per list per day) stores position; tasks without `order` sort first in array order. Completed tasks (their color is history) and project tasks (they inherit the project's list) may reorder but never change list; such a drop snaps back with a short message. Enforced in `moveTaskInDay` and mirrored in the UI.
- **Delete is instant with Undo.** Deleting a task or removing a routine has no confirm dialog; a 5 s toast offers Undo and restores the same ids/order. `lastDeleted` is in-memory only. Confirms remain for Delete project, Remove list, Erase all data.
- **The app starts empty on first run.** A fresh install has four default lists (Study, Career, Personal, Health) and no projects, tasks, or time blocks, so the owner's real data is never mixed with mock content. Sample data is available on demand from Settings ("Load sample data"), which replaces current projects/tasks/time blocks, and can be cleared again with "Erase all data." Both actions confirm first since they overwrite the owner's own data.
- **Data lives on this device, and syncs only if the owner signs in.** Persistence is zustand `persist` + AsyncStorage (`localStorage` on web); signed out, Settings says "Data is saved only on this device/browser". Calendar `events` are not persisted; feed events are re-fetched and sample events come only from "Load sample data".
- **Lists are user-managed and flat; removal archives.** After real phone use the owner wanted their own lists. Categories stay the entity name in code, the UI says "List". Removing a list archives it so past tasks and the Day Mark keep its name and colour; the last active list cannot be removed. Colours come from `colorKey`, not id, and the palette grew to six distinct colours (amber and teal added).
- **Per-list inline add replaces the bottom composer.** The owner chose a TodoMate-style input at the end of each list over a single "Add today's task" composer with a category picker. Enter adds and keeps the input open for rapid entry.
- **Routines are tap-to-add templates, not auto-generated.** They show as faint ghost rows in their list on today and future days (never on past days) until added; tapping the row creates a normal Task for the displayed day, tapping its circle creates it already done, and its `…` removes the routine (with Undo). This replaced the chip tray, which needed a second click into the add box. Routines are saved from a task's `…` menu or the add input's "Save as routine", and also managed on the List management screen. The default "Routine" list was renamed "Health" for new installs to avoid confusion with this concept.
- **Mobile web keyboard fixes.** Inputs are 16px (iOS Safari zooms smaller ones), the deployed viewport disables scale, and the phone bottom bar hides while typing instead of riding above the keyboard.
- **Email/password Supabase sync, local-first (owner request 2026-09-30).** The owner wants iPhone and desktop in sync. Choices: Supabase Auth with email + password (confirmation off) for simplicity; one generic `daymark_items` table of JSON rows with tombstone deletes instead of a table per entity, so the client model can change without migrations; per-item last-writer-wins by client timestamp rather than field-level merging, which fits a single owner on two devices; the app never requires an account, and signing in uploads this device's data and keeps devices in sync. Ids became random so devices cannot collide. Calendar events stay mock and unsynced. Reverses the earlier "no backend/auth in V0" rule for this purpose only.
- **Real calendars via iCal feeds and an Edge Function, not OAuth (owner request 2026-10-01).** The owner uses Google and Apple Calendar. Pasting each calendar's secret iCal link needs no OAuth consent screen, app verification, or token storage, and it works for Apple, which has no web calendar API. Browsers cannot fetch these links (CORS), so a Supabase Edge Function fetches and expands them (allowlisted hosts, size/time/range limits). The secret URL is stored only in the owner's RLS-protected `calendarFeeds` preference row. Feeds are strictly read-only: Daymark never writes to a calendar, and events never automatically become Tasks (the explicit "Add to Today" is unchanged). Trade-offs: feeds can lag minutes to hours (Google and Apple refresh them on their own schedule) and there is no write-back; Google OAuth is the later route if write-back is needed. All-day events travel as dates, not instants, so they never shift days across timezones. With no feeds connected the app shows no events.
- 2026-10-01: Motion uses RN Animated (JS driver for SVG/layout, native for opacity/transform) with shared `motion` tokens and a reduce-motion hook; the Day Mark tweens via one Animated.Value driving state, large mark only. No new dependencies.
- 2026-10-01 (owner): **Folder ordering and Today's strip.** One selector orders folders: pinned (manual `order`), then dated by deadline (overdue first), then undated (manual `order`); archived are excluded. Today's strip shows every active folder in that order (owner request 2026-10-02; previously only pinned and dated), then an "All folders" link. Pinned cards show a small pin glyph before the title. Pinned and undated folders reorder by drag (and a `…` Move up/down fallback); dated folders order by deadline.
- 2026-10-01 (owner): **Completion prompt.** A folder with a deadline whose steps are all done shows an inline "All steps done · Archive · Keep" (no modal, no celebration); Archive sets `status: 'archived'` with an Undo toast, Keep sets `completionAcknowledged` until a new step is added. Folders without a deadline never prompt; empty ones show "Nothing left · Add".
- 2026-10-01 (owner): **Source label.** Tasks that belong to a folder show a muted folder-icon + folder-name line under the title.
- 2026-10-01 (owner): **History is preserved through `missedOn`, not by auto-moving.** The first idea (clear folder tasks' past `scheduledDate` automatically) was replaced: a past day must keep showing what was left undone (3 of 5 stays 3 of 5). When an incomplete task is moved off a day before the real today, that day key goes into `Task.missedOn`; the day's mark and count (still the single `selectDayOrbit`) treat it as planned and not completed, and the past day lists it as a muted row ("→ moved to Oct 3" / "→ back in <folder>"). Moving a task off today or a future day is a plan adjustment and records nothing. This does mean moving tasks never shrinks a past day's denominator, which is deliberate; moving tasks forward does still change the future day's count, consistent with the existing move / remove-from-day behaviour. Nothing auto-moves: the banner on the real today ("2 unfinished from yesterday": Bring to today / Back to folder / Leave, dismissals remembered for the last 14 days in `carryoverDismissed`) covers plain and folder tasks left on the most recent past day within 7 days.
- 2026-10-01 (owner): **Tasks can move into folders.** From the task `…` menu ("Move to folder…", with "New folder…") or by dragging a task onto a folder card in Today's strip. The task takes the folder's list, leaves its day (a miss is recorded only when it came from a past day), shows an Undo toast "Moved to <folder>", and completed tasks cannot move. Drag-and-drop now covers Today tasks, dropping on folder cards, and folder reordering on the Folders tab.
- 2026-10-01 (owner): **Faster desktop drag.** With a mouse, a drag starts after ~120 ms or 4 px of movement (touch keeps the ~300 ms long press); `cursor: grab` / `grabbing` on web. A mouse press-and-release in place stays a click (edit title, toggle check, open a folder).
- 2026-10-01 (owner): **Doodle Day Mark style.** The owner requested a fourth selectable style, Doodle, inspired by the app icon's organic ring: same selector data and fill semantics as the other styles, drawn on a squashed hand-drawn loop.
- 2026-10-01 (owner): **Day Mark styles are selectable; themes come later.** Settings → Appearance lets the user pick the Day Mark style (Watercolor wash default, Soft ribbon, Glass vessel, Classic); it syncs as a preference. Whole-screen styles wait for a theming refactor (tokens are currently resolved at load). A future themes system may offer premium cosmetics; for now every style is free and there is no paywall UI.

- 2026-10-02 (owner): **Doodle is the default Day Mark; the app icon is the t1 lowercase d.** New installs start with Doodle (existing stored choices are kept). The icon is the open, slightly squashed lowercase d whose colors flow peach → rose → lavender → sky; the desktop rail brand mark uses the same icon.
- 2026-10-02 (owner): **Schedule sits with today's tasks; the new-folder list preselects the last used.** In the column layout the Schedule is a compact collapsible block atop the tasks column (no left-column Schedule, no third column). The New folder composer preselects the list of the most recently created folder (else the first active list). A per-step list override may come later.
- 2026-10-02 (owner): **Lists get many more colors.** Beyond the twelve presets, lists can use a 12-hue x 3-tone preset grid or a custom HSL color (saturation 25-95%, lightness 38-78%); the soft, ink and mark tones are derived from the hex. Stored as optional `Category.color`; classic `colorKey` presets still work and old lists are unchanged.
- 2026-10-02 (owner): **A missed task completed later is shown as resolved later, not as that day's completion.** A past day keeps its miss in the count, but once the moved task is completed on a later day the day shows it as a lighter band in the same category color, "1 of 3 · 1 later" in the count, and "✓ Done Oct 2" on its muted row. The completion rule is unchanged: `completion` still comes from `completedAt` on that day only, so late completions never count as that day's completion (the later day counts them normally). The moved task also shows a muted "From Oct 1" / "From Sep 29 · moved 3×" line on the day it now sits on.
- 2026-10-02 (owner): **Day Mark speaks about what was done, not a ratio.** The "x of y" count, the sentence "Completed on this day, kept by category.", the all-lists legend and the "Your day mark" lab link are removed; the area shows "n done" (with " · n later" on past days) and a per-list breakdown of that day's completions. The task list header still shows "n left".

- 2026-10-03 (owner): **Moving unfinished work off today also records the miss.** Previously only moves off past days set `missedOn`; a task pushed from today to tomorrow and later finished left no trace on its original day. Now any move of an incomplete task off today or a past day records that day, so every day it sat on shows it, and the later completion appears as that day's lighter 'later' band. Moves off future days remain plan adjustments.
- 2026-10-06 (owner): **Sending work from today back to its folder (or "Remove from day") un-plans it.** It leaves today's list entirely and records no miss; only moving it to another date leaves a "moved to" trace. Stale today-entries from before the fix are cleared on load.
- 2026-10-03 (owner): **Fully completed days carry a calm "All done" sign.** A day where every planned task was completed on that day shows "All done" instead of the count and a tiny check by its date in the calendars; no streaks, badges or new animation.
- 2026-10-04 (owner): **Drawn Day Marks: experiment first.** The owner wants users to draw their own mark shape; it lives only in `/mark-lab` for now (no store, sync or Appearance). Intended to be free during beta; a later "one free, more paid" model is recorded as a direction, not a commitment.
- 2026-10-04 (owner): **Drawn marks ship as Custom (beta).** The experiment graduates to a selectable fifth Day Mark style, Custom (beta), with its own drawing screen (`/draw-mark`) and a synced `customMark` preference; free during beta, pricing later. The lab route is renamed `/mark-lab` because Expo Router strips the `/daymark` base URL by string prefix and broke in-app navigation to `/daymark-lab` on GitHub Pages.
- 2026-10-04 (owner): **Drawn marks allow multiple strokes.** Free drawing (a smiley, a flower, initials) replaces the single stroke. The day fills the strokes in the order they were drawn, by arc length across all strokes concatenated, with category colors continuing across stroke boundaries. Stored as `customMark.strokes` (max 16 strokes, 240 points in all, normalized together); the first stroke stays in the legacy `points`/`closed` fields so older app versions still read something. Old single-stroke marks load unchanged; no persist version bump.
- 2026-10-04 (owner): **First run centers on drawing your Day Mark.** New users get a short two-step `/welcome` flow (brand + animated Doodle ring, then draw a mark or skip); signing in is not required. Feature explanations arrive later as contextual tips, not as tour screens. The route is `/welcome` (never a name starting with `daymark`, because of the base-URL prefix stripping). Existing users, anyone with tasks, folders, routines, a drawn mark or a non-default style, are marked done on migrate and never see it.
- 2026-10-06 (owner): **Reflection shows only completions.** The `/reflect` screen (week and month) counts what was done and never unfinished work, percentages, comparisons, streaks or scores. It is entered from Today and the Calendar header, not a new tab.
